Welcome to the World of Pokémon!

Step into the iconic Kanto region, where every legendary trainer begins their journey. Guided by the research of Professor Oak in Pallet Town, trainers set out to explore diverse habitats, build lifelong friendships with their partner Pokémon, and complete the PokéDex.

How Your Journey Begins

At age ten, aspiring Pokémon Trainers receive their very first partner Pokémon from Professor Oak's Laboratory. Whether you prefer the resilience of Grass-types, the fierce determination of Fire-types, or the steady composure of Water-types, your choice shapes the battles ahead.
